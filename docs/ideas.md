# Product ideas & roadmap notes

Ideas captured for later. Nothing here is implemented unless marked ✅.

## Google Search Console sync

| # | Idea | Status |
|---|------|--------|
| 1 | **Manual sync for now** — user clicks "Sync" per property; pulls the last 90 days (daily totals, queries, pages). | ✅ Current behaviour |
| 2 | **Longer sync periods** — let the user choose a longer range, up to GSC's 16-month maximum, so 90-day comparisons and year-over-year views work and history is saved before Google drops it. | 💡 Later |
| 3 | **Automatic pulls** — the app syncs from GSC on its own on a schedule (e.g. daily, last few days only), building history beyond 16 months with no manual clicks. | 💡 Later |

_Added 2026-10-15._
